# Identity Atlas community conduct

Identity Atlas is intended to be a practical and respectful community project. Participation is open to people with different roles, experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected conduct

1. Discuss the technical work rather than the person.
2. Give specific, useful and proportionate feedback.
3. Assume honest intent while still checking security and technical claims.
4. Respect privacy and never request live tenant evidence in public.
5. Acknowledge mistakes and correct them.
6. Make space for less experienced contributors to ask questions.
7. Follow the maintainer’s decision when a discussion has been resolved.

## Unacceptable conduct

1. Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
2. Publishing another person’s private information.
3. Sharing credentials, tenant data or confidential customer material.
4. Deliberately misleading security claims.
5. Repeated disruption after a maintainer has ended a discussion.
6. Spam, undisclosed promotion or unrelated solicitation.

## Moderation

Control Alt Delete Tech Bits may edit, hide or remove content that breaches these expectations. A contributor may be warned, temporarily restricted or blocked when proportionate to the behaviour.

Report conduct concerns privately to Mark@controlaltdeletetechbits.co.uk with the subject `Identity Atlas conduct report`. Include links and enough context to review the concern. Do not copy confidential tenant information into the report.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. A decision will consider the evidence, the effect on the community and any previous behaviour.
